Research

Swarm control at the Synergetic Adaptive Machines Lab, University of Michigan, since June 2025.

ICRA 2026 Accepted paper
Co-first author

The first real-world implementation of the 3D swarmalator model.

Swarmalators couple two things robots usually handle separately: where an agent is, and what internal state it's in. Everything that had been shown before lived in simulation. We put it on hardware, and added Control Barrier Functions so the guarantees hold when the space is full of obstacles — safe, scalable, fully distributed control with no central planner.

Getting there meant real-time control software written against the Crazyflie API, and a ROS network carrying VICON motion capture data back into the loop, closed across nine drones flying at once.

Alongside the flights, I built an NVIDIA Isaac Sim framework for the stochastic collision dynamics of smarticles, which let us observe formations at scales past 100 robots that we couldn't fly.

Honorable Mention, CRA Outstanding Undergraduate Researcher Award — the national award for undergraduate computing research.
